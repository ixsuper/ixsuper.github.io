Frequently asked.

What is Upcomin'?

Upcomin' is a release tracker for iOS 26 that follows upcoming and released games, movies, TV shows, and anime. It surfaces launch dates, countdowns, and trailers — all wrapped in a sunset-bright Liquid Glass UI.

Is it free?

Yes. The free tier lets you track up to 20 releases, set reminders, and use one Lock Screen widget. Pro unlocks unlimited tracking, the Global Launch Hub, Apple Intelligence guides, and more.

Does it support anime, movies, and TV?

Yes — all four categories ship at launch: games, movies, TV series, and anime. You can filter your feed by category or follow them all at once.

What is Apple Intelligence used for?

Apple Intelligence powers AI Guides (spoiler-light tips and walkthroughs), Daily Vibe (a personalized daily summary), and instant blurbs about a title. Everything runs on-device — your data never leaves your phone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Upcomin' → Cancel. Your Pro features remain active until the end of the current billing period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.
