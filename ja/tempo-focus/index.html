使い方

  1. Pick your apps. Tap Block, choose the apps and websites that pull you in.
  2. Set a session. 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes — or enable Pomodoro for a full work/break loop.
  3. Start. The picked apps are shielded, the Live Activity counts down, and the AI coach is one tap away.
  4. Stay. Done? Confetti. Streak grows, achievements unlock, charts update.

プライバシー

Tempo Focus stores everything on-device and in your private iCloud database. The AI coach runs entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence — no network calls, no API keys, no third parties. Zero analytics, zero tracking.
